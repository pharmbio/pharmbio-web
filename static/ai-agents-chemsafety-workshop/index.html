About the workshop

This one-day workshop brings together researchers, regulators and industry scientists to look at how LLM-based AI agents can support chemical safety assessment. Agents that search literature and databases, run predictive models and assemble evidence are moving from demos into real hazard and risk workflows.

We cover what works today, where the limits are, applications and case studies, and what regulatory use would require.

Aims

  • Give an overview of current agentic AI systems for toxicology and chemical risk assessment
  • Show working examples, from data retrieval to read-across and weight-of-evidence reports
  • Discuss validation, transparency and reproducibility of agent outputs
  • Identify what industry and regulators need before agent-generated evidence can be used in assessments
  • Connect groups working on these questions in Sweden and Europe

Questions and answers

Does it cost anything?
No. Attendance is free of charge. Lunch and coffee are provided for on-site participants who register.
Do I need programming experience?
No. The talks and the demo are aimed at a mixed audience of researchers, risk assessors and students.
How do I join online?
Register and choose Online via Zoom. The Zoom link is emailed to registered online participants the day before the workshop.
Can online participants ask questions?
Yes. Talks and the panel are streamed, and you can post questions in the Zoom chat. You can also send a question in advance in the Discussion section of this page.
Will the sessions be recorded?
This will be announced before the workshop. Links to slides and recordings will be added to the programme if they are made available.
I registered but can no longer come. What should I do?
Please email the organisers so that lunch can be planned for the right number of people.
